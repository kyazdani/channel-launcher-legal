Privacy Policy

How Channel Launcher handles data.

Channel Launcher is a TV guide app for Android TV, Fire TV, and Apple TV. This policy explains what data the app stores, sends, and uses.

Effective date: 6 September 2026

No accounts

Channel Launcher does not require or offer sign-in, registration, or a user account. Nothing you enter is tied to your name, email address, or identity.

Settings stored on your device

Channel favourites, hidden channels, channel order, theme, region, clock format, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your device unless you enable optional sync.

Optional cross-device settings sync

If you choose to link devices, the app generates random link and device identifiers and stores your selected settings in Supabase, a third-party database provider. The app also stores the device name and platform so linked devices can be identified in the Linked Devices list.

Sync is optional and off by default. You can unlink a device at any time. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it except with Supabase acting as our storage provider.

Analytics and remote configuration

Channel Launcher uses Firebase Remote Config to deliver app configuration and Firebase Analytics to understand app usage, such as which channels users try to open and which platform is being used.

Channel Launcher does not use advertising identifiers, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking.

TV listings data

Programme schedules shown in the app are broadcast schedule information from TV listings providers and are not personal data about you.

Children's privacy

Channel Launcher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Sync is optional. You can unlink devices from Settings. To request removal of an optional sync record, contact us through the Channel Launcher support page.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, email support@kazyaz.com.
