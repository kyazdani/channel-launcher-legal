Support

Need help with Channel Launcher?

For help with the TV guide, channel data, settings, device sync, or privacy requests, email us and we will help as quickly as we can.

Email support@kazyaz.com Open a support issue

What to include

Please include your device model, app version, and a short description of the issue. If the problem relates to a channel or programme, include the channel name and roughly when it happened.

Keep sensitive details private

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue. If you need to share private information, email us instead.
